Kenshira Novo

(The New Discovery)

Miri: "Shira, Taaq! Nos chena-shi finali! Ashi shu gai?"

Taaq: "Shira, Miri. Quam mi gai, mi habeta pen-nega. Nos na-kenita res nos visishi."

Miri: "Pen-nega bon esti pro Kenshian. Mano, datu de nos skana-vata musli walita. Re na-nega."

Taaq: "Wali lu mi de datu-bon, tunc. Res nos skanale?"

Miri: "Loka bota esti, ka ima la tera. Rena lan esti Hu-wisha. La... nos visale Tana-manye zun set."

Taaq: "Tana-manye? ...Re esti kenshira bota! Se datu bon, tunc nos debe chena."

Miri: "Bon! Nos ope-shi ka u weli. Pro kenshi! Pro Set-Tana!"


Use the below only to verify. No shortcuts. Keep in mind word order is different from English to HK, so it will take time to become fluent.


English Translation

Miri: "Hello, Taaq! We will travel finally! Are you happy?"

Taaq: "Hello, Miri. Although I am happy, I have negative-thoughts (doubts). We do not know what we will see."

Miri: "Doubts are good for a scientist. But, the data from our sensors speaks strongly. It is not unfavorable."

Taaq: "Talk to me about the good-data, then. What did we scan?"

Miri: "The world is large, with water and land. Its atmosphere is suitable for humans. And... we saw signs of civilization from the stars."

Taaq: "Civilization? ...That is a great discovery! If the data is good, then we must travel."

Miri: "Good! We will operate with one heart. For understanding! For the Star-People!"
