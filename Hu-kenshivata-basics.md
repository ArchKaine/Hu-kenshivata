## Hu-kenshivata Reference Guide (v1.5)

### I. Core Philosophy

Hu-kenshivata is a constructed language designed for clear, logical, and unambiguous communication. Its primary goals are:
* **Clarity & Conciseness:** To express complex ideas in a straightforward manner, minimizing misunderstanding.
* **Logical Structure:** To use a consistent and predictable grammar, primarily through compounding and affixation.
* **Neutrality & Accessibility:** To serve as a potential universal language, easy to learn and free of cultural bias.
* **Organic Growth:** To be a living language that evolves with the needs of its community.

### III. Grammar

#### A. Sentence Structure
The standard word order is **Subject-Object-Verb (SOV)**.
* *Example:* `Mi Set-Via visata.` (I the starship see.)

#### B. Nouns & Modifiers
1.  **Adjectives:** Adjectives **always follow** the noun they modify.
    * *Example:* `Set-Via bota, zuk.` (A starship great, fast.)
2.  **Unique Names:** A proper name for a specific object is placed **directly after the noun**.
    * *Example:* `Set-Via Stargazer.` (The starship *Stargazer*.)
3.  **Brands & Classes:** A brand, model, or class is linked to a noun using the preposition `de` ("of").
    * *Example:* `Tera-Vata de Nomad.` (A ground-vehicle of the *Nomad* class.)

#### C. Verbs & Tenses
Verbs are conjugated for tense using a simple suffix system.
* **Past Tense Suffix:** `-le` (e.g., `walile` - spoke)
* **Present Tense Suffix:** `-ta` (e.g., `walita` - speaks)
* **Future Tense Suffix:** `-shi` (e.g., `walishi` - will speak)

#### D. State of Being
The verb `esti` is used to equate two nouns or to emphatically state existence. In simple adjectival descriptions, it is often omitted.
* **Verb:** `Esti` (to be, to exist)
* *Example:* `Re esti veza, mano re esti veza de Kama.` (It is peace, but it is an enemy's peace.)

#### E. Mood & Modality
Modal helper verbs precede a main verb's root form to express possibility, obligation, etc.
* `Pote` (can, could, might) - Expresses **possibility**.
* `Debe` (must, should) - Expresses **obligation**.
* **Structure:** `[Subject] [modal] [verb root].`
* *Example:* `Nos debe chena.` (We must travel.)

#### F. Adverbs
Add the suffix **`-li`** to an adjective to turn it into an adverb that modifies a verb.
* *Example:* `len` (slow) → `lenli` (slowly). `Re chenata lenli.` (It travels slowly.)

#### G. Pronouns
1.  **Personal Pronouns:**
    * `Mi` (I, me)
    * `Shu` (you - singular)
    * `Re` (it)
    * `Nos` (we, us)
    * `Vos` (you - plural/formal)
    * `Eos` (they, them)
2.  **Possessive Adjectives:**
    * `Mina` (my, mine)
    * `Shuna` (your, yours - singular)
    * `Rena` (its)

#### H. Affixes & Word Formation
1.  **Prefixes:**
    * `Na-`: Negation ("not", "non-"). *Example:* `Na-Hu` (Not-Human).
    * `Shu-`: Repetition ("again", "re-"). *Example:* `Shu-wali` (Speak again).
2.  **Suffixes:**
    * `-an`: Denotes a profession or role. *Example:* `Tekmiran` (Engineer).
3.  **Compounding:** The primary method for creating new words. *Example:* `Set` (Star) + `Via` (Traveler) = `Set-Via` (Starship).

#### I. Questions
1.  **Yes/No Questions:** Place the marker `Ashi` at the **beginning** of a statement.
2.  **Interrogative Words:**
    * `Quen?` (Who?)
    * `Res?` (What?)
    * `Loka?` (Where?)
    * `Kenshi?` (Why?)

#### J. Clauses & Conjunctions
1.  **Coordinating Conjunctions:**
    * `La` (And)
    * `Mano` (But, however)
    * `Vel` (Or)
2.  **Subordinating Conjunctions & Clauses:**
    * `Pro` (Because, in order to) - Expresses cause or purpose.
    * `Se [condition], tunc [result]` (If... then...) - Expresses condition.
    * `Quam [concession], [main clause]` (Although...) - Expresses concession.
    * `Ke` (that, which, who) - Relative pronoun that introduces a descriptive clause.
        * *Example:* `Naken, ke Oracle nami, walita.` (The AI, who is named Oracle, spoke.)

#### K. Prepositions
* `De` (Of - belonging, association)
* `Zun` (From - origin)
* `Ki` (In)
* `Ka` (With)
* `Lu` (To)

#### L. The Imperative Mood (Commands)
To issue a command, use the verb's root form.
* *Example:* `Shena datu lu mi.` (Give data to me.)
